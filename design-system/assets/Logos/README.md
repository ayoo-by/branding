# Logos

The Ayooby eagle: a minimal monoline double-headed eagle after the Eagle of Saladin, with a faintly tinted heraldic shield. Vector is the source of truth.

- `eagle-light.svg`: sage `#51613f` (light `sage-700`) on a beige `#f3f0e7` (light `bg`) square; shield tint `#51613f` at 10%. For light contexts that need a self-contained tile.
- `eagle-dark.svg`: sage `#a7bf8f` (dark `sage-600`) on a navy `#0f1622` (dark `bg`) square; shield tint at 13%. For dark contexts.
- `eagle-mark.svg`: transparent, single ink in `currentColor` (shield tint at 14%). Embed it inline and set CSS `color`: `sage-700` in light, `sage-600` in dark. Shown through an image tag it has no colour to inherit and renders black.
- `eagle-favicon-light.svg`: transparent, `#51613f` (tint 14%). Source for the light favicon.
- `eagle-favicon-dark.svg`: transparent, `#a7bf8f` (tint 14%). Source for the dark favicon.

Reproduce the marks unmodified, in these colours, at natural proportions and with clear space. Do not recolour, redraw, animate or crop them. They are trademarks of Ayooby Ltd (see LICENCE.md in the branding repository).
